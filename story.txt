Barnaby the cat was convinced that the red laser dot on the living room rug was an alien invader that needed to be stopped. He crouched low, 
wiggled his fluffy hindquarters with absolute focus, and launched himself into the air. He landed perfectly on the bright red spot, 
only to find nothing but his own paws.
Confused but undeterred, Barnaby decided that brute force was not the answer. He crept silently behind the sofa, pretending to groom his ear while keeping a watchful eye on the carpet. When the dot unexpectedly reappeared on the wall above him, he sprang like a coiled spring and batted triumphantly at the wallpaper. The dot vanished once more, leaving Barnaby proud and convinced he had single-handedly saved the household from an alien invasion.
