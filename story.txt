Barnaby the cat was convinced that the red laser dot on the living room rug was an alien invader that needed to be stopped. He crouched low, 
wiggled his fluffy hindquarters with absolute focus, and launched himself into the air. He landed perfectly on the bright red spot, 
only to find nothing but his own empty paws beneath him.
Undeterred, Barnaby shook his head, pricked up his ears, and immediately began scanning the room for the elusive invader. Deciding that brute force was no longer the answer, he crept silently behind the living room sofa, pretending to groom his ear while keeping a watchful eye on the carpet. When the red dot unexpectedly flashed across the wall above him, he sprang like a coiled spring and batted triumphantly at the wallpaper. The dot vanished once more, leaving Barnaby proud and convinced he had single-handedly defended the household from an extraterrestrial menace.
